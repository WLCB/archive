Members shared many of these works at weekly meetings held between October and May for over 50 years. The goings-on of these meetings has been reconstructed, based on the thousands of pages of detailed minutes and meeting programs that have been preserved. You can learn more about these meetings here:

{% include index/carousel.html title="More than 1,000 publications . . ." height="300" %}

Here, you can explore the varied writings produced by the WLCB, which we have collected into a "Virtual Library" meant to recreate the now-lost library that was carefully maintained by the Club throughout its existence. These publications included novels, children's books, poems, histories, scholarly research, newspaper journalism, jokes, and plays.
