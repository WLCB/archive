## Eliza Butler (Mrs. Edward, Jr.) Stabler (1841-1918)

Eliza B. Stabler was was born in Ohio and married Edward Stabler, Jr. in 1865. Edward was a coal broker and served as an officer in the Maryland Academy of Sciences. It was Edward, in fact, who was first associated with the Club, as he finalized the agreement allowing the WLCB to meet in a room of the Academy of Sciences in 1891. In May 1895, after some discussion about the Club’s relations with the Academy of Sciences, Eliza was invited to join the Club, but once she was admitted, she was a very active member, serving as the Chairman of one of the Club's most popular committees, the Committee on Unfamiliar Records, from 1903-1915. Stabler was a member of the Daughters of the American Revolution and took an interest in the reform politics of the Arundell Good Government Club and the National Civil Service Reform League.
